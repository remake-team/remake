    How ReMake works

    1. Search for the itemSpelling mistakes and everyday words like “shopper” or “tetra pack” still work.
    2. Pick an idea that fitsCompare difficulty, time, cost and CO₂ saved, or sort by any of them.
    3. Follow the steps safelyEvery idea lists materials, tools, steps and a safety note.

    Where the numbers come from

    CO₂ saved is the item’s weight multiplied by a factor from the US EPA’s WARM model (v16). The factor is the difference between sending that material to landfill and recycling it.

    These are estimates. The factors are based on US data, and some materials have no factor, so no figure is shown for them. Costs are estimates in Pakistani rupees.
