    The evidence

    How the numbers work

    CO₂ saved is the item’s weight times a published emission factor: the difference between sending that material to landfill and recycling it. We use the US EPA’s WARM model (version 16), and UK government factors for clothing and textiles, which WARM does not cover. We found no published factors of this kind for Pakistan, so we use these widely cited international ones and label every figure as an estimate.

    What each idea shows

    • The weight used and where that weight came from
    • The factor used for that material
    • How the rupee cost was priced
    • Links to every source

    What we don’t claim

    These are estimates. WARM factors are based on US data, so real savings in Pakistan will differ. Where no trustworthy factor exists, we show no figure instead of guessing. Weigh your own item for a closer number.

    Main sources

    • US EPA WARM v16: management practices
    • US EPA WARM v16: containers and packaging
    • UK DESNZ conversion factors 2025
    • WRAP: textile reuse
